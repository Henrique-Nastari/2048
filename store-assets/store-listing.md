### Full description
```
Merge the numbers and reach 2048! 🎮

2048 Classic is the puzzle that took over the world, now with a modern look,
exclusive themes and smooth gameplay.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe in any direction to move the tiles
• When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one
• Reach the 2048 tile to win — and keep playing to beat your high score!

FEATURES
✅ 100% offline — play anywhere, no internet needed
✅ 3 visual themes: Classic, Dark and Neon
✅ Smooth animations and responsive controls
✅ Haptic feedback on merges
✅ Auto-save — close the app and continue right where you left off
✅ Personal best score always in sight
✅ Lightweight and fast: installs in seconds

Easy to learn, hard to put down. Download now and start merging! 🧩
```
